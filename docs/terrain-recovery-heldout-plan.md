arms, controller differences limited to the selected recovery flags, correct

and intact planning/review limits. Audit the saved evidence without simulating.

For each candidate seat compare completed recoveries and sorties, native ship
losses/rebuilds, controller-blocked time, pilot deaths, ownership and match
