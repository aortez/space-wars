Recent interventions and changes
    How to read this run
    Shells are injected every two seconds toward surviving material, independent of player survival. Scripted cuts use the normal queued terrain-edit path. Match victories are bypassed when necessary to keep physics advancing; actors and fragments are never reset or culled by the runner.
    Material conservation, cache revisions, terrain body/collider identities, support state, and finite physics values are audited once per simulated second. Tick advancement is checked every step. Runs that exhaust their wall-clock budget are marked incomplete and retain their partial evidence; they are not passes. The wall limit is checked between ticks and cannot interrupt a physics call that never returns. Performance overruns remain visible even when correctness checks pass. A finite speed large enough to cross the world diameter in one tick is flagged as an anomaly, without imposing a new gameplay speed limit.
    Same-build hashes aid repeatability checks. Cross-machine physics determinism is not guaranteed. These workloads do not validate terrain-aware AI, controller hardware, or long-session display performance.
