Method and limitations

2D, unit-depth MLS-MPM with quadratic transfers, Hencky elasticity and cohesionless Drucker–Prager yielding. The control sets the internal friction angle to zero; ground friction stays 0.6. E = 2000, density = 1, ν = 0.2; grid spacing 0.25, initial sample spacing 0.125. Explicit adaptive substeps. These are experimental parameters, not calibrated dirt.

A 288-point stream pours until 2.3 s. A 494-point bank loses half of its raised support at 2 s. A 1024-point bed receives radial velocity impulses at 2 and 5 s (radius 1.5, peak speed 12). Both blasts use the same location in the moving ground frame. This is a prescribed impulse, not explosive pressure or energy conservation. No cohesion, moisture, hardening, terrain deposition, or two-way rigid-body coupling yet.

Particle count and material mass are audited every 60 Hz step. Playback captures every third step. Replay compares every step’s state hash on the same build and architecture. Timings include input events and solver work; exclude audits, recording, replay, and drawing. The camera follows ground motion. The planetary base translates and rotates while gravity points toward its center.
