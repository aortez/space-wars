Dirt blast experiments

Recorded Rust simulation. Compare crater removal, rigid pieces, and rounded grains carrying the same terrain material. The pulse spreads the same radial velocity-change budget over several ticks.

“Above surface” counts loose cell centers more than one cell above the original surface. “Supported & slow” requires a contact path to ground and motion below 0.2 units/s relative to it; it is an instantaneous diagnostic. Counts are cells.

Grains preserve cell mass and inertia, using smaller round collision footprints that introduce pore space. These are experimental contact models; pressure, cohesion, compression, and deposition are absent. Simulation timing excludes audits, recording, replay, export, and browser rendering.
